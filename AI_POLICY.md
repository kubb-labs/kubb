# AI Contribution Policy

This project **allows all types of contribution to be made with the assistance of AI tools**.

For example, you may use an agent to generate code, documentation, issues, and other such content. As long as the contribution follows the project's contribution guidelines, it is welcome whether it was created by a human or an agent.

## Summary

- AI may be used when contributing
- AI usage does not need to be declared
- Contribution guidelines must still be followed
- Abuse, such as spam, scams, or malicious content, is not allowed

This policy is based on the [AI Allowed policy, version 1.0.0](https://ai-policy.dev/policies/ai-allowed/1.0.0) from 2026-09-20. Read more about this and other AI contribution policies on [ai-policy.dev](https://ai-policy.dev/).
