Open the long-running commands with the Kubb mascot: `kubb generate`, `kubb studio` (connect and login), and `kubb add`, `kubb update` and `kubb init` while they ask questions. `kubb generate` keeps its animated wake-up; the others draw the still mascot at once. Quick commands, `--yes` and `--dry-run` runs, and JSON output print no mascot. The banner names the running command, such as `kubb studio connect`.
